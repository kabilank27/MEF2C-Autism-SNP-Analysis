Results

1. Variant Prediction

Four selected MEF2C missense variants (R15P, L38P, L38Q and C39R) were evaluated using computational prediction tools.

2. Protein Stability

I-Mutant 2.0 and MUpro predictions indicated decreased protein stability for all four variants.

3. Functional Effect Prediction

MutPred2 scores ranged from 0.918 to 0.976, suggesting potentially important functional effects that require further investigation.

4. Structural Stability

DynaMut2 predicted destabilising effects for R15P, L38P and L38Q, while C39R showed a stabilising prediction.

5. Additional Analysis

Protein structure modelling, molecular docking, 100 ns molecular dynamics simulations and protein interaction analysis were included in the project.

Note

These findings represent computational predictions and should not be considered experimental proof of disease causation. Detailed numerical results and figures are documented separately and should be interpreted with appropriate validation.
