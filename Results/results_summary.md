
# Results Summary

## Project
In-silico analysis of selected missense variants in the MEF2C gene associated with autism.

## Variants Analysed
- R15P
- L38P
- L38Q
- C39R

## Analysis Performed
- Protein structure modelling
- Molecular docking
- Molecular dynamics simulation
- Protein-protein interaction analysis
- Structural and functional analysis
