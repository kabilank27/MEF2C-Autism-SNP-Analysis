Detailed Analysis Workflow

Step 1: SNP Retrieval

MEF2C gene SNP information was collected from genetic databases.

Step 2: Missense Variant Identification

Missense SNPs were identified and selected for further computational analysis.

Step 3: Functional Prediction

Selected variants were evaluated using computational prediction tools to investigate their possible functional effects.

Step 4: Protein Stability Prediction

Protein stability changes caused by amino acid substitutions were investigated using prediction tools.

Step 5: Protein Structure Modelling

Protein structures were modelled using I-TASSER for further structural analysis.

Step 6: Molecular Docking

Molecular docking was performed to investigate possible molecular interactions.

Step 7: Molecular Dynamics Simulation

Molecular dynamics simulations were carried out using GROMACS for 100 ns to study protein structural behaviour.

Step 8: Protein-Protein Interaction Analysis

STRING and GeneMANIA were used to investigate protein interaction networks.

Step 9: Structural and Functional Analysis

Structural changes and their possible functional implications were examined using structural analysis and visualization tools.

Step 10: Result Interpretation

The computational results were compared and interpreted to understand the potential effects of the selected MEF2C variants.
